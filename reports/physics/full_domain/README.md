## Full-domain vs Pin Point — the key result: **Pin Point is not representative of the domain**

| Parameter | Pin Point | Full domain | Reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Manning's n** (median, current→NWI) | 0.061 → **0.110** (54.7% cells changed) | 0.040 → **0.040** (47.0% cells changed) | Marsh-roughness correction is **concentrated in the coastal/marsh fringe** (where Pin Point sits). Domain median is unchanged because the domain has far more upland; the median cell is still upland. The correction is real but *spatially localized* to the coast. |
| **Ksat** (median mm/hr, POLARIS vs SSURGO) | 57.6 vs **100.8** (SSURGO higher) | 28.7 vs **20.9** (SSURGO lower) | **Sign reversal.** Pin Point soils are sandier/more permeable than the domain average; SSURGO reads higher there but lower domain-wide. |
| **Storage** (median mm, POLARIS vs SSURGO corrected) | 206 vs **137** (~1.4×) | 201 vs **37.7** (~5×) | Domain area is **dominated by a few low-storage soil units** (31–38 mm), so the area-weighted SSURGO storage falls well below POLARIS. The POLARIS/SSURGO agreement seen at Pin Point does **not** generalize. |

## Calibration implications
- **Manning:** the NWI marsh-roughness upgrade matters most exactly where the model is being
  applied (the Pin Point coastal fringe) — a targeted, high-value correction there, largely
  cosmetic in the upland interior.
- **Ksat & storage:** POLARIS and SSURGO **disagree in different directions in different
  places** — Pin Point sandy/high-storage vs a low-storage domain average. So a global "swap
  POLARIS for SSURGO" is the wrong move; the right move is to decide per-region, and the Pin
  Point clip (the actual model domain) is where the reconciled numbers (Ksat ~SSURGO-higher,
  storage ~1.4×) apply.

## Caveats
- The full-domain storage median (37.7 mm) is **area-weighted** and dominated by whichever
  SSURGO unit blankets most of the domain — worth a one-line spot-check of that dominant
  MUSYM (likely a coastal sand or tidal/water unit) before leaning on the 5× number.
- A cosmetic `Mean of empty slice` warning comes from downsampling all-NaN (water) blocks for
  plotting; stats are computed on full resolution.
- 28 SSURGO MUKEYs domain-wide, all with valid `sat_storage_mm`.
